## Quick one, worth it

Taum Sauk gets written off as a drive-up high point, and fair enough — the
actual summit is a plaque in the woods a few hundred feet from the parking lot.
But the walk down to **Mina Sauk Falls** is the real reason to come.

The trail drops about 450 feet over rocky, root-laced tread. Nothing technical,
but wear real shoes — the igneous rock gets slick when wet, and wet is exactly
when you want to be here.

## The falls

Mina Sauk is Missouri's tallest waterfall at 132 feet, falling in a series of
cascades down a volcanic rock face. In dry weather it's barely a trickle. After
rain it's legitimately impressive. We hit it two days after a storm and had the
ledges to ourselves for an hour.

If you want more, the **Ozark Trail** continues past the falls toward Devil's
Tollgate, a slot in the rhyolite you walk straight through. Adds about 2 miles
round trip.
